Pay Commitments

Select commitments to pay, pause, archive or edit them. Status comes from their due dates and the payments in the Expense Log.

Archived commitments are left out of every total until restored. Their past payments stay in the Expense Log.

Events, Tasks & Notes

Event = a day to remember. Task = something to tick off. Note = free text, no date. Events and tasks can repeat every N days, weeks, months or years, with an optional end date.

–
The end date is before the start date.

Settings

Saving

Auto-save

Either way, actions are saved straight away: Mark paid / pending, Pause, Archive, Save row, Delete and the Save button of the edit form.

Appearance

Privacy

Mask amounts and payment names

Amounts show as AED •••• and names keep only their first letters (Re••••), in lists, totals and charts. Same as the eye button in the header. Only how this device shows the data changes; exports and backups keep the real values. Opening a row to edit it shows the real values.

Private cloud backup

Backs up the saved database to your own Google Drive and/or OneDrive. It goes into a hidden app-only folder that this app alone can open (it cannot see your other files), straight from this device — nothing passes through a server, and nothing is public even though this website is. A latest copy plus 14 daily copies are kept.

Back up automatically

Right after Save, Mark paid and other actions; a few seconds after typed edits. Only while the app is open and signed in.

Sign-ins last about an hour (Google) to a day (OneDrive). If the app was closed for longer, open it and tap Reconnect once; backups resume. Only saved data is backed up. OneDrive redirect address to register:
