Pay Commitments

Each commitment is active between its start and end date; status is calculated from its due dates and the payments linked in the Expense Log.

Archived commitments are left out of every total, chart and list until you restore them. Their past payments stay in the Expense Log (as actual spend).

Pause leaves this month’s due date out of the dashboard (it comes back by itself next month) · Archive leaves it out of everything until restored.

Expense Log

Actual date = when it was spent · Commitment date = the planned due date it settles (linked payments count toward commitment status).
